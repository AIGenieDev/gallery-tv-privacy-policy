Privacy Policy for Gallery TV: Photo Slideshow

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Gallery TV: Photo Slideshow ("the app") is provided by Silicon Genie ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): used only to display your photos and videos in the gallery and slideshow on your TV.
  • Media location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): used only to read location information embedded in your photos so it can be shown on screen. It stays on your device.
  • Audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): used only to play music from your device during slideshows.

Your media is read and displayed locally on your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or sold. If you delete a photo or video in the app, the deletion is carried out on your device through the Android system.

Preferences

Settings such as favorites and slideshow options are stored locally on your device only.

Third parties

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services, and does not share data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
